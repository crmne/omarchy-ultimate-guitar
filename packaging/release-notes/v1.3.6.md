A safety fix: a misbehaving album-art server can no longer exhaust the shell's memory. Nothing changes in how the reader looks or works.

**Install** with `omarchy plugin add https://github.com/crmne/omarchy-ultimate-guitar.git --enable --yes`, or update an existing install with `omarchy plugin update crmne.ultimate-guitar --yes`.

## Fixed

- **The cover in the reader header can no longer take the shell down.** The reader used to hand the cover URL from the player straight to Qt, which holds a whole https response in memory before decoding it, however large. While the reader is open, covers are now downloaded by a small helper with a hard limit of 16 MiB and 20 seconds into a private cache under `~/.cache/omarchy/ultimate-guitar/artwork`, and the reader loads them from there. Covers the player keeps on disk are shown only when they are regular files within the same limit, and the cover URL stays off the process list. Reported by @HANCORE-linux in [omacom/omarchy-plugin-marketplace#12069](https://github.com/omacom/omarchy-plugin-marketplace/issues/12069). By @crmne.

## Known limitations

- A cover larger than 16 MiB is not shown; the header shows the title and artist on their own.
