Privacy and hardening fixes from the marketplace review: the plugin now looks a song up on Ultimate Guitar only when you open the reader, and its helpers are tighter about where they connect and what they read. The reader looks and works as before.

**Install** with `omarchy plugin add https://github.com/crmne/omarchy-ultimate-guitar.git --enable --yes`, or update an existing install with `omarchy plugin update crmne.ultimate-guitar --yes`.

## Fixed

- **What you play reaches Ultimate Guitar only when you open the reader.** Every track change used to send its artist and title to Ultimate Guitar, even with the reader closed. Now nothing is sent until you open the reader, which then looks the song up; a song looked up before still shows at once from the cache. Switching instrument or refreshing while the reader is closed happens the next time it opens. By @crmne; thanks [HANCORE-linux](https://github.com/HANCORE-linux) for the report.
- **A redirecting page can no longer grow the tab helper without limit.** Python's standard library reads the whole body of a redirect before following it, before the helper's own size limits apply. The tab helper now closes that response first, as the album-art helper already did. By @crmne; thanks [HANCORE-linux](https://github.com/HANCORE-linux) for the report.
- **A player can no longer make the shell reach services on your machine or network.** The player chooses the cover URL, so opening the reader could fetch from anything reachable over HTTPS, such as a local service. The album-art helper now connects only to public internet addresses: loopback, private, link-local and carrier-grade NAT addresses are refused before any connection is made, on every redirect hop, and the address checked is the one connected to. By @crmne; thanks [HANCORE-linux](https://github.com/HANCORE-linux) for the report.
- **Qt only ever opens the helper's copy of a cover on disk.** The helper used to check a player's cover file and then hand Qt the player's own path, so a file swapped in between could reach Qt unchecked. Covers on disk are now read once and written into the plugin's private cache, and Qt loads that copy. Every cover is also checked to be a PNG or JPEG of at most 3072 pixels a side and rewritten without its metadata, so a small file cannot cost gigabytes to decode. By @crmne; thanks [HANCORE-linux](https://github.com/HANCORE-linux) for the report.

## Known limitations

- Opening the reader for a song not looked up before now takes a moment while the tab is found.
- Covers served from your own machine or local network no longer show, and covers are not fetched through a proxy set in the environment.
- Covers larger than 3072 pixels a side, and covers in formats other than PNG and JPEG (such as GIF or WebP), are no longer shown. The tab is unaffected.
