The first tagged release of Ultimate Guitar Tabs: the tab for whatever you are playing, one click away in the Omarchy bar. It picks the track up from your media player, finds the best-rated tab on Ultimate Guitar, and renders it in the shell, so there is nothing to search for and no account to make.

**Install** with `omarchy plugin add https://github.com/crmne/omarchy-ultimate-guitar.git --enable --yes`, then `omarchy bar move crmne.ultimate-guitar --section right --before omarchy.tray`.

## New

- **The tab for the song that is playing.** The plugin reads the track from any player that speaks MPRIS, searches Ultimate Guitar, and picks the best-rated tab, weighing each rating by how many votes stand behind it. Remaster and featuring suffixes are tidied out of the title first. By @crmne.
- **Guitar tab, chords, bass, ukulele and drums.** Pick the instrument in the reader and it sticks for the next song. A search returns every type at once, so switching costs no extra request, and a song nobody tabbed for bass or drums falls back to guitar, with the header naming the type you got. By @crmne.
- **Every version, one dropdown away,** each with its rating and vote count. By @crmne.
- **Rendered in the shell, columns aligned and chords colored,** with auto-scroll at a speed you set for when both hands are on the guitar. Scrolling by hand stops it. By @crmne.
- **Big enough for the whole room.** Step the text size up or down, or expand the reader to the size you set in the bar settings. The instrument, text size, scroll speed and expanded state are remembered. By @crmne.
- **Official tabs open in your browser.** They are Guitar Pro-style files only Ultimate Guitar's own player can show, so the reader offers an **Official tab available** button instead. By @crmne.
- **Keyboard and script control.** `omarchy-shell crmne.ultimate-guitar toggle` opens and closes the reader, with `show`, `hide`, `refresh`, `open`, `official` and `status` beside it, ready to bind in Hyprland. By @crmne.
- **Careful with what the network sends.** The fetch helper only follows `https` on `ultimate-guitar.com`, re-checking every redirect. Pages are size-limited before and after decompression, results and tab text are capped, and the shell collects at most 2 MiB of the helper's output. The cache is private, is read without following symlinks, and is written atomically. By @crmne.

## Known limitations

- Tabs come from Ultimate Guitar only, and a song it has no text tab for shows as having none.
- Official tabs cannot be shown in the reader, only opened in the browser.
- Needs Omarchy Quattro with its Quickshell-based shell, and `/usr/bin/python3` for the fetch helper.
